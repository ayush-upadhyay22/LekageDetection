Methods we may use

Thermal imaging

Infrared frames show surface temperature differences. Cooler or irregular patterns can mark zones worth checking for moisture or concealed plumbing — they do not reveal a pipe behind a wall on their own.

Moisture meter

Readings on walls, ceilings, and floors help compare dry and affected areas and track whether a stain is active or historic.

Acoustic detection

Listening equipment can pick up characteristic sounds associated with water movement in concealed or buried lines when site conditions allow.

Ultrasonic detection

Used selectively on concealed leakage investigations where acoustic contrast is useful. Not every site or pipe material yields a clear signal.

Visual inspection

A methodical physical review of plumbing, wet rooms, walls, ceilings, floors, terraces, and other accessible construction details remains the backbone of every visit.
